# Governance

## Model

RoboProbe uses a simple **maintainer model** (sometimes called "benevolent dictator" for small projects).

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|------|-----|-----------------|
| **Lead maintainer** | Priyanshu Jha ([@CodewithJha](https://github.com/CodewithJha)) | roadmap, final technical decisions, releases, merging, Code of Conduct enforcement |
| **Maintainers** | *none yet* — invited by the lead maintainer after sustained, high-quality contributions | review and merge PRs in their areas, triage issues |
| **Contributors** | anyone submitting issues, docs, code or reviews | follow `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the Code of Conduct |

## Decision making

1. Everyday changes: decided in pull-request review (lazy consensus; maintainer approval required).
2. Significant technical decisions: recorded as **ADRs** (`docs/adr/`); larger proposals go through an **RFC** (`docs/rfc/`, template in `docs/templates/`). The lead maintainer makes the final call after discussion, stating the rationale in the ADR.
3. Changes to evaluation methodology (`docs/19`) require an RFC, because they affect the comparability of research results.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent, high-quality contributions over several months, good review conduct, and agreement with the project's principles (free/open-source only, spec-first, reproducibility). Nominated and confirmed by the lead maintainer.
